title: "Iberian barbel"
summary: "A barbel endemic to the Iberian Peninsula, long-bodied with barbels by its mouth. A bottom-dweller. Also listed as Barbus bocagei."
how: "From bridges on sunny days with low water, foraging along the bottom. Polarised glasses help."
where:
  sico: "Arunca and Anços rivers, Arzila channels, the bridge near Conímbriga."
