---
title: "Bee orchids (Ophrys)"
summary: "Orchids whose lip mimics the shape, colour and scent of female bees and wasps. Males try to mate with the flower and carry its pollen to the next one."
how: "March to May. Search at ground level and stay on paths so you don't trample them. Never pick them: they do not survive transplanting."
where:
  sico: "Limestone meadows and open scrub on the plateau."
---
