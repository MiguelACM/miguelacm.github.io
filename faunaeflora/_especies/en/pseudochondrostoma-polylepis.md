---
title: "Iberian straight-mouth nase"
summary: "A fish endemic to the Iberian Peninsula, with a straight mouth adapted to scraping algae off stones. It runs upriver in shoals to spawn in spring."
how: "From bridges on sunny days with low water; polarised glasses help. Runs upriver to spawn from March to May."
where:
  sico: "Arunca and Anços rivers, Arzila channels, the bridge near Conímbriga."
