summary: "A small, slender carnivore with a spotted coat and ringed tail. It was introduced to Iberia many centuries ago, probably from North Africa."
how: "Nocturnal. Look for latrines: piles of droppings on rocks and high ledges."
where:
  sico: "Cliffs, limestone pavements and oak woods: Buracas do Casmilo, Rio dos Mouros."
