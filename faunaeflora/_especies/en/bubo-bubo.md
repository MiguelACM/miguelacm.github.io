---
title: "Eurasian eagle-owl"
summary: "Europe's largest owl, with ear-like feather tufts and orange eyes. A top predator that takes anything from rabbits to other owls."
how: "At dusk, listen for the deep \"ooh-hoo\", audible hundreds of metres away. Signs: pellets 7–10 cm long under rocks. Do not use recordings to call it in."
where:
  sico: "Breeds in the ravines of Vale dos Poios (Pombal). Older records from Rio dos Mouros."
---
