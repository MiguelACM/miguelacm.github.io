Within 40 km you get two very different kinds of birding: **raptors and rock birds** on the cliffs and limestone plateau of Sicó, and **waterbirds** in the reedbeds of Paul de Arzila. You can do both in one day: Arzila at dawn and the hills by mid-morning, when raptors start soaring on the thermals.

## Viewpoints

### Paul de Arzila
The most reliable site in the region. The 1.5–2 km circuit has a boardwalk, hides and a tower. The visitor centre opens on weekdays 9:00–13:00 and 14:00–17:30.

- **Spring:** purple heron (visible even from the visitor centre's balcony), little bittern, reed warblers.
- **All year:** marsh harrier from the tower in the morning; kingfisher near the wooden bridge.
- **At sunset:** night herons leave the willows.
- **Winter:** wintering ducks, November to February.
- **Autumn:** passage warblers and bluethroats.

### Top of Serra de Sicó
Access via Vale. Good for **short-toed eagle** from March to September, mid-morning to mid-afternoon, and for plateau birds in spring: wheatear, corn bunting and red-legged partridge.

### Vale dos Poios (Pombal)
Ravines where the **eagle-owl** breeds. At dusk in winter its deep "ooh-hoo" carries hundreds of metres. From April to September, noisy flocks of **alpine swifts** along the cliffs in the late afternoon.

### Buracas do Casmilo
Limestone crags where the **blue rock thrush** is likely, singing from the rock tops from February to June, early in the morning.

### Rio dos Mouros and Conímbriga
A limestone gorge with boardwalks. **Short-toed eagle** in summer, **kingfisher** in autumn and winter, and older eagle-owl records.

## When to go

| Season | What to expect | Where |
|---|---|---|
| December to February | Eagle-owls calling; wintering ducks | Poios at dusk; Arzila |
| March to May | Summer visitors arrive; herons nesting; raptors displaying | Arzila early; Serra de Sicó |
| June and July | Chicks and fledglings; alpine swifts | Arzila; Poios late afternoon |
| August to October | Songbird passage migration | Arzila |

## Local precautions

- **Avoid the cliffs from January to July**: at Poios, Buracas and Rio dos Mouros, people and climbing disturb nesting eagle-owls and alpine swifts.
- **Don't use recordings** to call in the eagle-owl.
- At Arzila, stay on the boardwalks and keep quiet.

> **Bonelli's eagle** and **red-billed chough** are not confirmed in Sicó. If you see either, log it on eBird or report it to ICNF, without revealing nest sites.
