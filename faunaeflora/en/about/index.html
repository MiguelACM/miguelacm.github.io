About

About this guide

This is a field notebook on the wildlife and plants of Portugal, organised by region. Its aim is practical: to help you know what to look for, where and at what time of year, and how to watch without disturbing.

Conservation status, where shown, is from the Portuguese red list cited in each region's sources. Timing and field-sign tips follow the general natural history of the Iberian Peninsula where there is no specific local record.

Illustrations

The illustrations are original, simplified drawings made for this guide. They give an idea of shape and colour, not a basis for rigorous identification. For that, use a field guide or reference photos.

Helping out

Many species are marked as probable because there is no published local record. If you see one, log it on eBird or iNaturalist with the date, approximate location and, if possible, a photo. Never publish nest sites of sensitive species.

Found a mistake? Let us know through the site's GitHub repository.
