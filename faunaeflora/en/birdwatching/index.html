Birdwatching

Portugal lies on the migration route between Europe and Africa, and packs mountains, estuaries, cereal plains and sea cliffs into a small country. That makes it one of the best places in Europe to watch birds all year round.

Here you'll find general guides for beginners and regional guides with viewpoints, seasons and target species.
